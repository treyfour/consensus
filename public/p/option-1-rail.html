Open Questions in CRISPR Off-Target Effects for In Vivo Therapies

Several unresolved questions persist across detection, biological variability, structural variants, and clinical monitoring for CRISPR off-target effects in in vivo therapies WIENERT 2022 KALTER 2025.

Large-scale aberrations — translocations, inversions and chromothripsis — remain far harder to detect than small indels using current workflows, which the field treats as a major unmet need HÖIJER 2021.

Human genetic diversity is an under-explored confound: variants at off-target sites differ across populations, so a guide validated in one reference genome may behave differently in another CANCELLIERI 2022.

Finally, there is no consensus on how long, and with what assay, patients should be monitored after in vivo delivery ANGELINI STEWART 2025 HUANG 2022.
