Five structures for curation inside a research thread

Same concept, same real data, five genuinely different containers. All five carry per-paper provenance, a note, the right-click verb set, multi-select with “Ask within these”, and a path to My Library — so the only variable is where the curated set lives and what the primary act is.

A · RailBeside the threadReviewing a listHow does curation happen without leaving the thread?
B · MarginIn the gutter, anchored to sentencesAnnotating while readingWhat if curation were a byproduct of reading?
C · BoardA 2D canvasArranging into themesWhere does understanding live, as opposed to evidence?

E · WeaveNoneActing where evidence is citedWhat if we added no new surface at all?
A · THE RAILspine

The Rail

The set accumulates in a panel beside the thread.

GIVES

Closest to today’s IA. Recurrence and decisions readable at a glance across the whole set.

COSTS

The set is a list, not a shape. No spatial thinking.

B · THE MARGIN

The Margin

Notes anchor to the sentence that produced them.

GIVES

Lowest-friction capture. The note arrives already linked to several sources at once.

COSTS

Weak at 40 papers and no opinions yet. Nothing to browse.

Open →
C · THE BOARD

The Board

Cards on a canvas, grouped until the gap is visible.

GIVES

Best for synthesis. A one-paper group is a visible thesis gap.

COSTS

Highest build cost. Real risk of reading as a different product.

Open →
D · THE LEDGERverbs

The Ledger

One decision at a time; the methods trail writes itself.

GIVES

Strongest on trust and reproducibility. Exclusion reasons become a methods section.

COSTS

Feels like work. Assumes a systematic-review mindset.

Open →
E · THE WEAVE

The Weave

No container. The citation chip is the control surface.

GIVES

Adds no new surface, which the brief explicitly favours. Evidence acted on exactly where it is cited.

COSTS

Curation is invisible until you click. Hard to see the set as a set.
