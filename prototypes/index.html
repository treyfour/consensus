Round 1 — where does the curated set live?

Five structurally different containers. All five carry per-paper provenance, a note, the right-click verb set, multi-select with “Ask within these”, and a path to My Library, so the only variable is the container and the primary act.

ROUND 8 · CURRENT DIRECTION

N · Twin, refined

Notes sits left of References. The composer is pinned to the top of Notes, so it never scrolls out of reach: type, press ⏎, and it completes into a card below, ready for the next thought. Newest first. And selecting prose in the answer then right-clicking gives Add selection to Notes, which carries the excerpt and the papers cited inside it, naming them in the menu before you commit.

 ROUND 7 · TWIN PANELS

M · Twin panels

Notes moves back to the right, beside References rather than beneath it: References next to the answer it belongs to, Notes outermost. With Notes closed, the Notes button itself becomes the drop target — it fills, scales and reads “drop to add”. Dropping shows a receipt hanging off the button with undo, and Add a note is offered rather than forced.

 ROUND 6 · CURRENT DIRECTION

L · Flanking

Notes stops living beneath References and moves to the opposite flank: Notes left, thread centre, References right, equal width. Symmetry is what makes them peers. Right-click any citation or source for Add to Notes or Add with a note…; a chip flies to wherever Notes currently is, and a toast confirms with View and Undo.

 ROUND 5 · CURRENT DIRECTION

K · Dock + Split

J and I combined. References and Notes open independently, so a researcher who wants notes visible while reading can close References entirely. When both are open the panel splits with a draggable divider. The dock appears whenever Notes is off screen and disappears when it is on, because it exists only to stand in for Notes. Citations in the answer are draggable too, so the dock still works with References closed.

 ROUND 3 · CURRENT DIRECTION

I · References → Notebook

References stay first-class at the top of the column; the Notebook sits underneath, so the drag is short and vertical. A note and an annotation are the same object: one pin makes it an annotation, no pins makes it a loose thought, several pins make it a claim with evidence. Drag a reference in, press ＋, or just start typing in the empty note.

 ROUND 4 · REVEAL / HIDE

J · The Dock

The pill is the collapsed Notebook, not a separate thing. Start dragging and it opens into an upload-style drop target; drop and an annotation field appears, then closes, all without leaving References. Click it to open Notes as a tab. One slot, two tabs, and a persistent bridge between them.

Round 2 — the transition into a safe space

Select a source, peek at it on hover, drop it somewhere safe, annotate it, query it later. Same gesture ladder in all three: hover peeks for free, ＋ keeps in one click, right-click opens the verb set. The variable is where the safe space lives.
