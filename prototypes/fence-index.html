Consensus · scope fence · five options

Am I still searching everything?

A researcher narrows to a few sources by adding them to the note. From that point on, retrieval should be limited to those sources — and the interface has to say so, or the researcher spends the rest of the thread unsure. The variable across these five is how the fence is communicated and how you get out of it. Same scenario, same data, same eject requirement in all of them.

THE SCENARIO, IDENTICAL IN ALL FIVE
  1. Eight turns already asked. References shows everything retrieved so far.
  2. Select three sources on the right, then Add 3 to note — and search only these.
  3. The fence is set. Three sources dim out; References says your 3 sources only.
  4. Ask again. The funnel says your 3 sources · nothing new retrieved, and retrieval really is limited.
  5. Eject. Everything comes back. The note and every turn survive.

In the funnel

GIVES
